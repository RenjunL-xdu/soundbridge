What can this device actually do?

Plays a sweep through the speaker and listens with the microphone, then measures signal quality at every frequency. Speakers and microphones vary enormously up near 20 kHz, so this is the fastest way to know what you are working with.

Set the volume to about two thirds. Keep the room quiet for a few seconds. Most of the sweep is inaudible; the lower part will not be.
